# Extreme Sweet Database

This platform provides computational and predictive resources for researchers working on sweetness perception, molecular design, and structure-activity relationships of sweet compounds.

Researchers in physics, chemistry, and related fields can access DFT-optimized geometries and vibrational spectra to extract structural parameters such as bond lengths, bond angles, and dihedral angles for use in conformational analyses or molecular docking studies. Vibrational spectroscopy researchers can compare calculated IR and Raman spectra across compounds or use the spectral data as references for experimental band assignments.

Cheminformatics and machine learning researchers can explore the sweetness prediction models, examine the roles of individual molecular descriptors in sweetness prediction through the integrated explainability analyses, and use the platform to generate predictions for new compounds from SMILES input.

Food scientists and medicinal chemists investigating next-generation sweeteners can use the regression models to estimate the relative sweetness intensity of candidate molecules and the classification model to screen compounds for sweet taste potential.

A molecular descriptor dictionary is provided to support the interpretation of descriptor-based model outputs. This resource will be progressively expanded to cover the full set of Mordred descriptors.

All data, molecular structures, spectra, and model outputs are freely accessible, interactive, and downloadable.
